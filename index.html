Mind the S-1: Anthropic is telling investors its own AI might do things nobody asked it to

Anthropic's S-1 warns that its models may take harmful actions no user directed, and that its safeguards may not always stop people from misusing them. The same document asks the public to buy shares. It goes on to describe a business that depends on scarce chips and power, defends copyright suits, has taken the Department of War to court, and leaves control with a trust and the seven co-founders.

View 03 · The loss

An $80.7 billion loss: $72.4 billion of it comes from the notes gaining value

The operating loss in the first half of 2026 was $693 million. Most of the rest sits below the operating line: a $72.4 billion non-cash charge for the higher fair value of convertible notes, derivatives and SAFEs. The filing ties the charge to increases in Anthropic's own valuation.

Beyond the money

View 05 · Who decides

Who decides: a trust picks the board majority and seven founders hold 50.1% of the vote

Anthropic is a Delaware public benefit corporation, so its directors must weigh a stated Company Purpose alongside returns to stockholders. The Long-Term Benefit Trust elects four of the seven directors. A single Class F share, held through the co-founders' LLC, carries 50.1% of the general vote. Nasdaq will treat Anthropic as a controlled company.

Litigation

Most of the open cases are about training data

  • Bartz (authors, books): settled for $1.5B plus interest, approved July 2026. Authors and publishers who opted out have filed their own suits. p.312–313
  • Concord I (song lyrics): cross-motions for summary judgment heard October 21, 2026. p.312–313
  • Music publishers including Universal, Sony and Warner Chappell: training and outputs; early stage. p.313
  • Reddit: narrowed in September 2026 to contract, interference and unfair competition. p.313
  • Four Canadian actions, including La Presse; consumer class actions over subscription advertising. p.313
Government and national security

An appeals court upheld one supply-chain-risk designation, 2–1

  • Federal agencies were directed to stop using the technology, and the Department of War designated Anthropic a "supply-chain risk" (letters of March 4, 2026). p.195
  • The N.D. Cal. largely granted summary judgment for Anthropic on August 27, 2026. p.195
  • The D.C. Circuit upheld the Section 4713 designation 2–1 on September 25, 2026; rehearing en banc requested. p.195
  • Commerce imposed worldwide export restrictions on Fable 5 and Mythos 5 in June 2026, later lifted. p.194
  • FTC and state attorney general inquiries. p.313
IP, data and privacy

The filing calls the law on training "uncertain"

  • How copyright doctrines and open-source licenses apply to model training "are uncertain". Risk 47, p.75
  • Web data collection may create liability or limit use of the data. Risk 51, p.79

  • Personal and sensitive data is processed for AI safety work. Risk 49, p.76
